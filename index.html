GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently
asked.

Quick answers to help you feel confident about the application process.

Can I apply to more than one programme?＋

Application limits vary by institution. For this demo, contact the admissions team to confirm whether multiple programme choices are permitted.

Which documents should I prepare?＋

Typically, applicants need academic transcripts or mark sheets, proof of identity, a recent photograph and any programme-specific documents. Check the exact checklist for your chosen course.

Are scholarships available?＋

Scholarship availability and criteria depend on the institution and programme. Use the application form to request information about merit-based or need-based support.

Can I edit my application after submitting it?＋

Changes are handled by the admissions office. Contact the institution promptly with your application reference and the correction required.

How will I know if I am admitted?＋

Universities usually communicate decisions through the applicant portal or the email address provided in the application. Follow the official instructions for accepting an offer.
